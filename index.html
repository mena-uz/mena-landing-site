Questions

How does Mena score my face?

AI reads your three photos and rates skin, brows, hair, jawline and posture from 1 to 10. Your potential counts only what care, habits and style can change.

Is this medical advice?

No. Mena is a self-care plan. If we notice something worth checking, we pause that area and suggest you see a doctor first.

What happens to my photos?

They stay on your phone. For analysis they are sent encrypted, processed and deleted. They are never used to train AI.

How much time does it take?

You choose in the quiz. Most days take about ten minutes, in any order.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your subscription is managed by the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel in your phone settings.
